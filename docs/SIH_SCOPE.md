# SIH Prototype Scope

## In scope
- Student mobile-style workflow
- Geo-fenced attendance UI and verification simulation
- Digital clinical/industrial logbook
- Supervisor approval
- Skill Passport
- Explainable AI-assisted matching
- Institution dashboard
- Industry dashboard
- Ministry analytics dashboard

## Out of scope for prototype
- National identity integration
- Production NIC deployment
- Real patient records
- Full national onboarding
- Enterprise HR integrations
- Historical model training at national scale
- Payments
- Blockchain credentials
- Full multilingual rollout

## Core success path

1. Student receives internship assignment.
2. Student verifies attendance at the approved site.
3. Student submits practical activity.
4. Supervisor verifies the activity.
5. Verified hours and competency appear in the Skill Passport.
6. Industry posts a vacancy with required skills.
7. Matching engine ranks candidates.
8. Recruiter reviews evidence and decides.
9. Institution and Ministry views aggregate anonymized outcomes.
